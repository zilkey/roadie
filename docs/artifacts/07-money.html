Money

Projects are the buckets; income, expenses and payouts land in them. Income comes to the band, payouts go to people, and owed vs paid works the same both ways. Totals are worked out when read.

Supersedes the ledger design (#26). Built in #39. The one ledger collection, settle, payouts owed on the deal and income lines on the deal are replaced by projects, income and payouts. Accounts, balances and transfers were cut: a payment records a method and a date.

Talk to Roadie

Built first. What you can say to Roadie in the app, or to Claude, Gemini or another assistant through the MCP server, and what it writes or answers. The commands behind these exist before any form does.

Spending money6

We owe Eric $42 for gas.

writesAn expense in Eric's name, category travel, pay back on. The same record Eric would make himself. It shows under We owe Eric.expenses.add

I just spent 50 on gas.

writesAn expense in your name on tonight's show and its project. Nothing to ask unless the amount is missing.expenses.add

We paid Eric $100 for Friday's show.

writesA payout to Eric, kind pay, on Friday's show, paid today. Asks how once (Venmo, cash...) if you didn't say.payouts.add

We owe Dana $200 for the show.

writesA payout to Dana with no paidAt, so it sits under We owe until someone marks it paid.payouts.add

Pay Eric back for gas.

writesLists Eric's open pay-back expenses and the total, then on a yes marks them paid back and writes one reimbursement payout.expenses.payPerson

Mark Dana's $200 paid, Venmo.

writesSettles the open payout with a method and today's date.payouts.markPaid

Money in3

Lost Lake still owes us the $400 guarantee.

writesIncome, category guarantee, from Lost Lake, no receivedAt: owed to us. Attached to the Lost Lake show if one matches.income.add

Lost Lake paid.

writesFills in receivedAt on the owed income (today unless you say a date).income.setReceived

Bandcamp sent $86 for October.

writesIncome from Bandcamp with no show, received today, on Band unless you name a project.income.add

Projects4

Put the $750 video shoot on the Lighthouse video project.

writesAn expense (category video) with projectId matched by name from the open projects. Leave the project out and it lands on Band.expenses.add

Start a project for the new album.

writesCreates a project of kind album. Dates and a budget are optional; Roadie adds what it finds and doesn't refuse over blanks.projects.create

Close the Fall 2025 run.

writesCloses it: still readable, nothing new can land on it. Projects are closed, never removed.projects.close

Make the Duo gig a Duo project show.

Looking back4

Who do we owe?

How Roadie behaves

  • MCPAdds what it finds, even when optional fields are blank; asks once, with a list, only for what is required (an amount, who).
  • MCPMoney writes show a confirm line before they run, and payouts and paying back read the total back.
  • MCPAdmins only: income, payouts, projects and logging an expense in someone else's name. A member's Roadie can only write their own expenses.
  • MCPAmounts are cents. A closed project takes nothing new.

Read views

Built with the commands, so people can see what Roadie and the MCP server did. Updates show live.

A show's Finances tab

  • adminSee the show's project, set by its configuration (Full band: Band; Duo: Duo).
  • adminSee Money in: each line Received or Owed to us.
  • adminSee Paid out: each payout Paid or We owe, with Mark paid.
  • adminSee the show's expenses, with who paid and whether the band owes them.
  • adminSee the net card: in, paid out, expenses, net to the project, still owed to us, we still owe.

The Money page

  • adminSee In, Out and Left over the year.
  • adminSee Owed to us, each line with what it was for.
  • adminSee We owe grouped by person, each with a Pay button.
  • adminSee Recently: the latest income, expenses and payouts, each with its project.
  • adminBrowse by Overview, Projects, By show or By category.

Projects

  • adminSee every project with In, Out, Net and Budget.
  • adminOpen a project: budget spent, in so far, net, we owe, every record on it, spent by category.
  • memberSee the project names, kinds and dates, so an expense can pick one. No totals.

My money

  • memberSee what the band owes me.
  • memberSee my expenses: waiting, paid back or no pay back.
  • memberSee Paid to me: payouts made to me that are paid.

Editing by hand

Forms come after, generated from the same schemas. New records need an explicit Add or Save, and Cancel throws the draft away. Existing records edit in place and autosave; there is no page-level Edit.

Money in (modal)

  • adminChoose Owed to us or We got it.
  • adminPick what for, who from, the amount, the project, an optional show.
  • adminWhen we got it, add how and when. Nothing else is asked.
  • adminRead the sentence back, then Save. Cancel throws the draft away.

Money out (modal)

  • adminChoose We owe or We paid, then the kind: pay back something they bought, pay for playing, another band's cut, other.
  • adminPick who (band people first, then contacts), amount, what for, project, optional show.
  • adminWhen we paid, add how and when.
  • adminRead it back with a line on what it saves: pay back is an expense in their name, everything else is a payout.

Owed and paid

  • adminTap Received on income that came in, or Mark paid on a payout.
  • adminSplit evenly on the Paid out card fills one unpaid payout per player on the show (net divided by players); edit each before saving.
  • adminPay a person from We owe: opens Money out set to We paid with their open items.

Projects and shows

  • adminAdd a project with an explicit Add; Cancel discards the draft.
  • adminClose or reopen a project. Edit its name, dates and budget in place.
  • adminChange a show's project from its Finances tab.

Expense form

  • memberAdd an expense with a Project picker (open projects; defaults to the show's or rehearsal's project, else Band).
  • memberKeep The band should pay me back, or untick it for the band's own money.
  • memberChange or remove my own expense until it is paid back.
  • adminEdit any value in place; changes autosave.

Shown when

What stays hidden until it's needed in this module.

  • shown whenIf the Money section is off in Settings, the Money tab, a show's Finances tab and every amount are hidden, and Roadie's interviews skip money.
  • shown whenA member's Money starts in More as My money: their own expenses, what the band owes them, Paid to me.
  • shown whenIncome, payouts to other people, project totals and the net card never reach a member.
  • shown whenThe Project picker on a form lists open projects only; closed ones stay readable.
  • shown whenThe Project card on a show is admin-only and sits on the Finances tab.
  • shown whenSplit evenly shows only when the show has players who have no payout yet.
  • shown whenHow and When appear only once an item is paid or received.
  • shown whenA tour or album, when built (#11, #12), is attached to a project; the project is what money hangs off.

Hidden never means blocked. Roadie and the post-show interview skip questions about hidden fields and features that are off. If someone says it anyway, Roadie records it, or offers to turn the feature on.

How it works

  • Integer cents. Every amount is whole cents. No floats anywhere.
  • One project each. Every income, expense and payout belongs to exactly one project; none means Band. A new show takes its configuration's defaultProjectId and an admin can change it. A new record defaults to its show's project, else the default. A closed project takes nothing new; a project is closed, never removed.
  • Owed vs paid, both ways. Income with no receivedAt is owed to us. A payout with no paidAt, or a pay-back expense with no paidBackAt, is what we owe. payBack: false is the band's own money: nothing owed.
  • No accounts. A payment records a method (cash, Venmo, check...) and a date, not which account. How much is in the bank is the bank app's job.
  • Members handle their own expenses. They add, change and remove their own until it is paid back, now with a project picker. An admin can log one in a bandmate's name: "we owe Eric $42 for gas" is the same record Eric would make.
  • Paying someone back (expenses.payPerson) writes one reimbursement payout listing expenseIds and stamps each expense paidBackAt, paidBackBy and reimbursedBy.
  • Player pay is entered by hand as a pay payout per player. Split evenly on the Paid out card fills one unpaid payout per player (net ÷ players) for the admin to edit; no split rules are stored.
  • Computed when read. weOwe (by person), owedToUs, projectTotals and showTotals live in src/lib/money.ts. Project net = income − payouts − expenses over that projectId, owed amounts included. The one stored cache is showDeals.totals (income, paidOut, expenses), refreshed whenever a record on the show is written.
  • Roadie confirms money writes and reads back the total before paying.
  • Tours and albums attach to a project. Creating one later (#11, #12) creates its project; a project carries no tracks, routes or files.

Migration

  1. Run npm run migrate:money -- --dry-run against the emulator. It walks the real data through the writer overlay, writes nothing, and prints a "what won't land cleanly" list for sign-off.
  2. Run it against production once signed off (it needs --yes there). Projects Band (default) and Duo are created if missing and each configuration gets a defaultProjectId.
  3. Every show gets a projectId (its billed-as configuration's, else Band); every expense gets one (its show's, else Band). Legacy income categories on expenses are rewritten to other and reported.
  4. Each showDeals.income[] line becomes an income doc with the show, its project, from the venue and receivedAt the show date when it was received. The deal's income[] is left as legacy data and the totals refreshed, so every show's net is unchanged. A rerun is safe (keyed by sourceKey).

Words

Projectprojects. A bucket money belongs to; Band is the default.
Incomeincome. Money coming to the band; a show is optional context.
Payoutpayouts. Money from the band to a person.
Owed to usIncome with no receivedAt.
We oweUnpaid payouts and unreimbursed pay-back expenses.
ReimbursementA payout that lists the expenses it paid back.

Reimbursements count once. A reimbursement payout repays an expense that already counts as spent, so projectTotals, showTotals, moneyRows and showDeals.totals leave it out of paid out. It still shows in the lists.
