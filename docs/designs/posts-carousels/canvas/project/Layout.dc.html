Anyone in the band · Drive app or Roadie

Dump

Put photos anywhere under Posts › Ideas: a whole phone's worth in a folder named however you like, or loose files. Roadie's Upload button puts them there too.

  • Nothing to rename, sort or tag.
  • Photos and video both land here; HEIC from iPhones is fine.
2
An admin · Posts › Photos

Add to carousel draft

Pick photos in Roadie and press Add to carousel draft. Roadie makes Drafts › the draft's name and moves the photos into it.

  • A photo from outside Posts (a show folder, a poster) stays put; the draft gets a Drive shortcut to it.
  • Take a photo out of the draft and it goes back where it came from.
  • Delete the draft and every photo goes back.
3
Roadie, when the post goes out

Posted

Roadie renames the draft's folder with the day it posted, numbers the photos in carousel order, and moves it to Posted › that month.

  • Each photo is marked in Drive with the post it went out in, so Photos shows "Posted Nov 14" even on a shortcut's photo.
  • If the post fails, the folder stays in Drafts.

Rules Roadie keeps

  • Roadie moves and renames. It never deletes a photo.
  • It only empties and removes folders it made itself (a deleted draft's folder goes to Drive's trash).
  • Every move is in Drive, so the folders make sense without Roadie.
